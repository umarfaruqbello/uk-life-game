# 🇬🇧 UK LIFE - Life Simulation Game

UK LIFE — An exciting life simulation game where players build their lives in the United Kingdom.

### 💷 Game Features
- **Work & Earn:** Start as a Cleaner in London (£12/hr) and grow to CEO
- **Buy Properties:** From Birmingham Flat to Mansion in Kensington
- **Start Businesses:** Open a Fish & Chips shop, Uber business, or Premier League Club
- **UK Cities:** London, Manchester, Birmingham, Liverpool, Leeds
- **Live the UK Dream:** Pay rent, build credit, become a millionaire!

### 🎮 How To Play
1. Click "Work" to earn money
2. Buy properties to earn passive income
3. Start a business to become rich
4. Your goal: £1,000,000
